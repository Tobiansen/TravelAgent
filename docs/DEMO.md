# Two-minute demonstration

The story is: **share a ticket → observe a disturbance → inspect the bag → ask Exa for reporting steps → gather evidence → continue with the airline**.

The recorded movement is real. Any damage described for the demonstration is a separate, explicitly fictional traveler statement. No airline report is submitted.

## Before starting

1. Start `app/TravelAgent Demo.cmd` on the laptop and wait for **Laptop connection ready**. Keep the window open, laptop awake and phone on the same reachable Wi-Fi. The Exa key must already be configured; see [Setup](SETUP.md).
2. Fully close and reopen the phone app for a blank active trip. Keep it in **Real Bluetooth** and connect the board. Require current authorized state, then leave it **Disarmed** for the quiet motion story.
3. Have a suitable airline email ready in Gmail. Select the message text and use **Share → TravelAgent**. Share only the text needed for the demonstration, and avoid exposing passenger or booking information on camera.
4. Confirm there are no old photos, selected damage answers or previous motion records. New samples after connecting are expected to populate the new trace.

## On the clock

| Target | Action and narration |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Share the confirmation text from Gmail. Before immediately shows locally imported **Finnair / CPH → HEL** for the prepared case. “The app reads my flight details from text I share.” |
| 0:25–0:50 | Open Guard and gently move the Disarmed assembly. Show the white measured trace, tap a red Movement flag, then choose **Inspect bag and contents**. “This is real motion received over Bluetooth. It tells me to check the bag.” |
| 0:50–1:00 | Choose **Yes, I found damage** for the fictional case. “After inspecting the suitcase and contents, I record the damage I found.” |
| 1:00–1:30 | Tap **Find reporting steps with Exa**. Show the pending/result reveal, photo requirement, citation and actual cached/live label. “Exa researches the airline's reporting process and required evidence.” |
| 1:30–2:00 | Show **Take photo / Choose photo** and capture a new local photo if time allows. Open the available Finnair reporting page and stop at the public delayed/damaged selector. “I continue through the airline's own reporting process. Nothing has been submitted.” |

This is a rehearsal target, not a measured guarantee for the combined Gmail/BLE/Exa route. Network and browser load times vary. The older prepared-case route was completed within two minutes; that does not establish the timing of this final route.

## Honest fallbacks

- A saved result or fixture must keep its visible label. Do not describe a saved cached result as a newly completed request.
- Select **Demo before sharing** if real hardware is unavailable. Use **Guard → Presenter controls → New demo** to add labelled illustrative events, and keep that whole story in Demo. Its categorical events are not measured acceleration.
- A no-damage answer completes inspection without entering a damage-report flow.
- If the sourced handoff is unavailable, show the official instructions rather than opening an unverified destination.

Keep siren/drum and code-changing demonstrations outside this two-minute reporting story. Finish any guard demonstration with a board-confirmed Disarmed state. See [Hardware](HARDWARE.md) for the controls and physical limits.

## Words to use accurately

| Say | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| “Local ticket parsing” | The app identifies supported airline/route text; Exa is not involved here. |
| “Measured acceleration change” | Magnitude deviation from 1 g, not an event-supplied impact peak. |
| “Movement recorded” | A threshold marker prompts a human inspection; it does not prove damage or cause. |
| “Exa reporting research” | A real request retrieves sourced airline requirements; source freshness may still be cached. |
| “Connected-session timeline” | Disconnected handling is not recorded for later backfill by this firmware. |
